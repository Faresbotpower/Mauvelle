01What is Mauvelle?

Mauvelle is a soothing heat patch for your cycle. Individually sealed and beautifully kept, it brings a small comfort ritual to your day.

02Where do I place the patch?

Over clothing, at your lower belly or lower back. Never apply it directly to your skin. Always follow the instructions supplied with the product.

03Can I wear it while sleeping?

No. Only use the patch while awake, and remove it if it feels too warm or your skin becomes red or irritated.

04Can I use a patch more than once?

Each patch is for single use. Keep the sachet sealed until you are ready to use it.

05When can I get my hands on it?

We are preparing to launch in the UAE. Final pricing, pack size and delivery details will be shared at launch.

06How do I care for my skin?

Wear over clothing. Do not use on broken or sensitive skin, or with other heat sources. Remove if it feels too warm or causes redness or irritation. If you are pregnant, have diabetes, poor circulation or reduced sensation, consult a doctor before use. Keep away from children and do not open the inner pouch.
