# UAE setup and compliance

Not legal advice. Confirm each item with the authority or a licensed consultant.

## 1. Trade license

- **Dubai e-Trader license** (DET): about AED 1,370 a year, home-based, sells online and on social. Confirmed for **UAE and GCC nationals residing in Dubai**. Expats should check eligibility with DET before planning on it.
- **Free zone e-commerce license** (for example IFZA, Meydan, SPC, Shams): the usual route for expats. Allows importing and online trading, gives an import code. Compare price, visa allowance and whether the activity list covers "trading in personal care / health accessories" and "e-commerce".
- **Existing entity:** check whether SCC's license can add a trading / e-commerce activity. Usually cheaper than a new company, but keeps the brand inside the software entity.

## 2. Import

- Need an importer of record with a customs code (comes with most trading licenses).
- UAE customs duty is generally 5% on CIF value. Confirm the HS code with the broker (chemical heat packs often fall under 3824).
- Ask the forwarder whether the patches ship as general cargo by air and sea. Iron-powder heat packs usually do, but they confirm with the MSDS.

## 3. Product rules

- Sold as a **general consumer product** with comfort claims only. Keep it that way (`product/claims-register.md`).
- If marketed for pain relief or as therapeutic, it may need registration as a medical device with the Emirates Drug Establishment / MOHAP, which is slow and expensive.
- Ask the consultant whether any municipality product registration applies (Dubai Municipality Montaji covers some personal-care categories).
- Arabic labeling of mandatory information.

## 4. Trademark

- File with the Ministry of Economy. About AED 750 application + AED 5,000 registration per class (check current fees). Classes 5 and 10 first.
- Later: Saudi (SAIP) before entering KSA, or Madrid Protocol.

## 5. Selling online

- Shopify Payments or a local gateway (check what Shopify supports in the UAE at setup time).
- VAT registration is mandatory above AED 375,000 annual taxable supplies, voluntary above AED 187,500.
- Amazon.ae and Noon need the trade license, VAT certificate (if registered), brand registry needs the trademark.
