# Launch plan

Order matters: lock the product before spending on packaging and ads.

## Phase 1. Validate (weeks 1 to 4)

- [ ] Pick logo direction (A, B or C)
- [ ] UAE trademark search for MAUVELLE, classes 5 and 10 (agent, about AED 500 to 1,000)
- [ ] Buy domain (mauvelle.care or hellomauvelle.com) and grab @mauvelle handles
- [ ] Send RFQ to 5 to 8 suppliers (`product/supplier-rfq.md`)
- [ ] Order samples from the best 3 to 4
- [ ] Run the sample test (`product/sample-test.md`) and a wear test with 5 to 10 women
- [ ] Waitlist page live (the site prototype with a real email form) to test demand

## Phase 2. Set up (weeks 4 to 8)

- [ ] Trade license (see `uae-setup.md`)
- [ ] File UAE trademark (classes 5 and 10, maybe 3 and 35)
- [ ] Choose supplier, collect all documents and test reports, update `claims-register.md`
- [ ] GS1 UAE barcodes
- [ ] Final packaging artwork on supplier dielines, Arabic native review, printed proof
- [ ] Customs broker or 3PL that handles import clearance
- [ ] Fill `unit-economics.csv` with real quotes, set price

## Phase 3. First batch (weeks 8 to 14)

- [ ] Place order: start around 3,000 to 5,000 patches (500 to 800 boxes), or the MOQ
- [ ] Pre-shipment inspection (third party, about USD 300) or detailed photo/video QC
- [ ] Ship (sea is cheaper; check with forwarder whether air is fine for the patches)
- [ ] Product photography and short videos (box opening, wearing under clothes)
- [ ] Shopify store built from the prototype (`shop/shopify-plan.md`)

## Phase 4. Launch (week 14+)

- [ ] Waitlist launch email with launch pricing
- [ ] 10 to 20 UGC creators in UAE (gift a box, ask for honest videos)
- [ ] Meta and TikTok ads, small budget, test 3 hooks
- [ ] Amazon.ae and Noon listings once reviews and stock are stable
- [ ] Subscription (box every cycle) after first repeat orders show up
