# Mauvelle brand book

## Idea

Period care that feels like a skincare ritual. Warmth, kept beautifully. The brand is calm, never clinical, warm, never cute. It sits on a vanity, not in a drawer.

**Positioning line:** Soothing heat patches for your cycle, made to be worn unseen and kept in sight.

**Who for:** women 22 to 40 in the UAE and GCC who already pay for premium skincare and wellness, and want period comfort that matches. Second buyer: gift givers (partners, friends, mothers).

## Name

- English: **Mauvelle** (mauve + elle). Pronounced "moh-VEL".
- Arabic: **موڤيل**. Use ڤ for the V sound, the Gulf convention. Alternative if the market finds ڤ unfamiliar: موفيل.
- Always lowercase in the logo, title case in sentences ("Mauvelle patches").

## Logo

Direction A, "Moonrise", is the working logo: lowercase Cormorant Garamond with a crescent moon.

Rules:
- Clear space around the logo: the height of the crescent on every side.
- Minimum size: wordmark 80px wide on screen, 22mm in print.
- Plum logo on cream, lilac mist or lavender. Cream logo on plum or violet. Never on apricot.
- Never stretch, outline, add shadows, recolor outside the palette, or retype the wordmark.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| cream | #F7F1EA | Ground. Page and inner packaging |
| lilac-mist | #E6DDF2 | Raised surfaces, sachet back |
| lavender | #B9A6D9 | The brand. Box sleeve, sachet front, hero blocks |
| violet | #5B4690 | Buttons and links |
| plum | #3E2A4F | All text and the logo |
| plum-muted | #6B5A7B | Secondary text on cream and lilac-mist only |
| apricot | #E8A87C | The heat cue. One small accent per view |
| hairline | #D9CDE6 | 1px lines |

Print: give the printer the hex values and ask for their closest Pantone matches, then approve a physical proof before the run. Lavender shifts easily toward grey or blue on uncoated stock, so proof on the actual material.

Contrast: plum on cream 11.4:1, plum on lavender 5.8:1, cream on violet 6.9:1. Never set plum-muted on lavender (fails).

## Type

- **Cormorant Garamond** 400/500: headlines, product names, big numbers.
- **Manrope** 400/500/600: body, labels, buttons. Labels are uppercase, 11px, 0.14em tracking.
- **Noto Naskh Arabic** 400/500: all Arabic.
- All three are SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use including packaging.

## Shape and pattern

- Soft corners: 16px cards and sachets, pills for buttons.
- The motif is the moon: discs, crescents, half-rounds, a slow orbit.
- The heat cue is a single apricot dot or a soft apricot glow. Never flames.
- No shadows on flat UI; surfaces separate by color. Product renders may use soft shadows.

## Photography and imagery

- Daylight, cream linen, soft lavender shadows. Hands, a robe, a mug, a hot water bottle replaced by the box.
- Show the box and sachet on vanities, nightstands, in tote bags.
- Show bodies only clothed and relaxed: a woman curled on a sofa, at a desk, stretching. Never pained faces, never clinical settings.
- Avoid: red liquid, pain imagery, stock "cramp" poses, gradients of pink and purple.

## Layout

- Generous whitespace. Sections of 120px desktop, 80px mobile.
- One idea per screen. Big serif headline, short paragraph, one action.
- Motion is slow and floaty: drift, breathe, orbit. Nothing bounces.
