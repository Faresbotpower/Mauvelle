## Patch anatomy (site callouts)

1. **Cloud-soft cover.** A brushed non-woven that feels like fabric, not plastic.
2. **Slow-release warmth core.** Iron, carbon, vermiculite and salt react gently with air. Divided into cells for even heat.
3. **Air-regulated film.** Micro-perforations meter in just enough air to hold a calm, steady temperature.
4. **Hypoallergenic adhesive.** Holds to fabric through your day and lifts away clean.
5. **Peel-away liner.** Keeps the adhesive fresh until you are ready.

Note: we do not say "proprietary" or "patented". The formula is the OEM's.

## How to use

1. Tear open the sachet at the notch. The warmth begins on contact with air.
2. Peel the liner and press the patch onto your underwear, over your lower belly or lower back.
3. Remove after up to 8 hours.

## Warnings (required on pack, EN + AR)

Wear over clothing, never directly on skin. Do not use while sleeping. Do not use with other heat sources. Remove if it feels too warm or if skin becomes red or irritated. Do not use on broken or sensitive skin. If you are pregnant, diabetic, have poor circulation or reduced sensation, consult a doctor before use. Keep out of reach of children. Do not open the inner pouch of the patch. Single use.
