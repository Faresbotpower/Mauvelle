## Never use

- Relieves / reduces / eliminates pain or cramps
- Treats, cures, therapy, therapeutic, medical-grade
- Clinically proven, doctor recommended, gynaecologist approved (unless we commission real studies)
- Proprietary, patented, exclusive technology (the formula is the OEM's)
- Safe for everyone, no side effects
- 100% natural

Why: health and medical claims can make the UAE treat the product as a medical device needing registration (MOHAP / Emirates Drug Establishment), and Amazon and Meta ads review these words strictly.
