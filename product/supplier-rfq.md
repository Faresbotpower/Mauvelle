# Supplier RFQ

Where to look: Alibaba (filter: Verified Supplier, Trade Assurance, 5+ years), 1688 via an agent for lower prices, and Made-in-China. Search terms: "menstrual heat patch OEM", "period heating pad disposable private label", "warm patch for period cramps custom".

Shortlist 5 to 8 factories that already make **menstrual** patches (not only body warmers) and show a celled core. Ask all of them the same questions so quotes compare.

## Message to send

Subject: OEM menstrual heat patch, custom printed pouch and box, UAE brand

Hello,

We are launching a premium brand of menstrual heat patches in the UAE and are looking for an OEM partner.

What we need:
1. Disposable, air-activated menstrual heat patch with adhesive for underwear, segmented (celled) heat core, soft non-woven cover, about 95 x 130 mm.
2. Custom printed inner pouch with our artwork (matte finish).
3. Optional: printed folding box of 6 patches, or we print the box separately.
4. No fragrance, no herbal or drug additives.

Please send:
- Unit price per patch with custom printed pouch at 5,000 / 10,000 / 30,000 pcs
- MOQ for custom pouch printing, and for custom non-woven color if possible
- Price for a printed box of 6 if you can supply it
- Temperature curve (time vs temperature), average and maximum temperature, duration
- Thickness of the finished patch
- Adhesive specification and any skin irritation / hypoallergenic test report (HRIPT)
- Ingredient list, MSDS, shelf life
- Factory certificates (ISO 9001, ISO 13485, CE, FDA if any)
- Sample lead time and cost, mass production lead time
- Incoterms: FOB and DDP to Dubai, UAE

We would like samples of your standard menstrual patch first (5 to 10 pcs, plus any existing private-label pouches) sent to Dubai.

Thank you,
