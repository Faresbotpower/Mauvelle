# Testing samples at home

Test every supplier's sample the same day, same conditions. Score 1 to 5. Record in a sheet.

You need: a kitchen thermometer with a probe or a cheap IR thermometer, a timer, cotton underwear and a microfibre pair, a ruler or caliper.

| Test | How | Good result |
|---|---|---|
| Heat-up | Open, start timer, note minutes until clearly warm | ≤ 10 min |
| Temperature | Probe under the patch on a folded towel every 30 min | Steady, never painfully hot |
| Duration | Keep logging until it feels cool | ≥ 8 h warm |
| Hot spots | Feel all over at hour 1 and 3 | Even across the surface |
| Thickness | Measure unopened and worn | ≤ 3 mm |
| Flexibility | Bend and sit with it on | Bends with you, no crunch |
| Adhesion | Wear over cotton for a full day of moving | Stays in place |
| Removal | Peel off cotton and microfibre | No residue, no pilling |
| Smell | Smell on opening and after 2 hours | Nothing noticeable |
| Feel | Cover fabric against hand | Soft, not plasticky |
| Pouch | Tear notch, print quality, seal | Tears cleanly, sharp print |

Wear-test with 5 to 10 women you know before choosing. Ask: warm enough, too warm, comfortable, visible under clothes, would you pay [AED XX] for a box of 6.
