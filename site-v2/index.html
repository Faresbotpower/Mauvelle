How to wear it

Peel, place, settle in.

  1. 1

    Tear

    Open the sachet at the notch. The warmth core wakes on contact with air.

  2. 2

    Place

    Peel the liner and press the patch onto your underwear, over your lower belly or lower back.

  3. 3

    Settle in

    Feel the warmth build within minutes. Remove after up to 8 hours.

Please note. Wear over clothing, never directly on skin. Do not use while sleeping or with other heat sources. Remove if it feels too warm or if skin becomes red or irritated. If you are pregnant, diabetic, have poor circulation or reduced sensation, consult a doctor before use. Keep out of reach of children. Single use.
