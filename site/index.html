How to wear it

Peel, place, settle in.

  1. 1

    Tear

    Open the sachet at the notch. The warmth core wakes on contact with air.

  2. 2

    Place

    Peel the liner and press the patch onto your underwear, over your lower belly or lower back.

  3. 3

    Settle in

    Feel the warmth build within minutes. Remove after up to 8 hours.

Please note. Wear over clothing, never directly on skin. Do not use while sleeping or with a heating pad. Remove if it feels too warm. If you are pregnant, have diabetes, poor circulation or reduced sensation, ask your doctor first. Keep away from children.

Questions

Good to know

How warm does it get?

Comfortably warm, never hot. The air-regulated film keeps the temperature steady. If it ever feels too warm, take it off.

Where do I place it?

On the outside of your underwear, over your lower belly or lower back. Never directly on skin.

Can I sleep with it on?

No. Use it while you are awake so you can remove it if it feels too warm.

Is it reusable?

Each patch is single use. Once cool, it goes in your household waste.

Will anyone see it?

It is ultra-thin and flexible, so it disappears under most clothes.
