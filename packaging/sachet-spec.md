Mockup: `mockups/sachet.html`. Printed by the OEM on their barrier film. Ask for their artwork template (AI or PDF dieline) once the patch size is chosen.

- TEAR HERE at the notch
- Crescent + mauvelle wordmark
- موڤيل

## Back (lilac mist)

- Peel, place, settle in.
- 3 steps in English
- 3 steps in Arabic
- Short warning: Not for direct skin contact. Do not use while sleeping. Single use.
- LOT / EXP area (left blank for the factory's inkjet)

## Notes for the factory

- Matte finish. Soft-touch only if the barrier is not affected.
- Crimp seals top and bottom, tear notch top right.
- Colors: lavender #B9A6D9, lilac mist #E6DDF2, plum #3E2A4F, apricot #E8A87C. Request a printed proof.
- Leave a clear area for the batch code.
